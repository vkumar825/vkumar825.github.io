## Happy 4th of July! 🎉 🦅 🇺🇸 🦅 🎉

It's the 250th anniversary of the United States!

What better way to celebrate than with a review of a movie made for this day, Young Washington, as my first-ever blog post! 

{{< figure src="https://res.cloudinary.com/u0n8mltb/image/upload/v1783210717/young-washington_k5yuct.webp" alt="Young Washington poster" class="center" >}}

The movie is directed by Jon Erwin, and while I am not familiar with this director or his previous works, I do recognize some members of the cast, in particular Andy Serkis and Ben Kingsley.

What I wanted to get out of this movie were well-written characters, along with tons of action scenes and epic battles. After seeing it in the theater, I can say it met my expectations.

At the start, it began with an action sequence without any buildup, an ongoing battle, with young Washington abruptly awoken from his sleep. I could feel the tension and excitement as we saw him navigate through the smoke, cannons, and soldiers.

Then it transitioned into a flashback sequence. It was a little abrupt, but that aside, I was able to see the progression and development. The movie had established the starting point of his character arc, starting with his dream to become a British officer but being rejected due to his colonial background. 

This progressed to him managing to get a chance, though he suffered a major setback by the French forces, almost to the point of wanting to quit, following the loss of his half-brother, someone whom he deeply looked up to. And yet, thanks to his mother's encouragement, he still persevered against all odds. Overall, I really liked the development of Washington in this movie.

Plus, the action scenes were great; the battles presented were thrilling to watch, with muskets galore!

Overall, I found the movie very enjoyable to watch. Was it historically accurate? Probably not. It's a movie, and if it looks cool, then I'm all for it. I'd give this movie a full score, **10/10**. It's definitely a movie I plan to re-watch once it's available on DVD.
