The movie's directed by Jon Erwin, and while I am not familiar with him, I do recognize some members of the cast, namely Andy Serkis and Ben Kingsley.

I've heard about this movie from the trailer on YouTube, and it has been a while since I saw a historical film; the last one I ever watched was Dunkirk in 2017, which I really liked. Plus, it was going to come out on the 250th anniversary of the USA; I think it's very thematic to watch it on that day, too.

The trailer showed the things that enticed me into this film, which were drama and action, and with the inclusion of Andy Serkis and Ben Kingsley, I felt the movie would be a very entertaining experience. Having seen it in the theater, I can confirm it is indeed the case and has met my expectations.

In the opening scene, it started with an ongoing battle (Battle of the Monongahela), and young Washington was abruptly awoken from his sleep. I could feel the tension and excitement as we saw him navigate through the chaotic environment with smoke, cannons, and soldiers everywhere.

From there, it transitioned into a flashback sequence. I felt that it was a little jarring because he was recalling his past right in the middle of the battlefield, but that aside, I was able to see the progression and development.

The movie established the starting point of his character arc, starting with his dream to become a British officer but being rejected due to his colonial background. Even when he managed to get a chance to prove himself to the authorities, he experienced a major setback from the French forces, following the Battle of Fort Necessity. This, coupled with the loss of his half-brother, whom he really looked up to, led him to the point of wanting to quit and give up.

However, because of his mother's words of encouragement, he managed to pull himself back up, rejoin the military, and persevere against all odds, as observed in the climax of the movie. I really liked the character development of Washington in this movie.

The action scenes were great; the battles presented were thrilling to watch, with muskets galore!

Overall, I found the movie very enjoyable to watch. Was it historically accurate? Probably not. At the end of the day, it's a movie, and if it looks cool, I'm all for taking creative liberties.

I don't think the movie's perfect, as some points felt a bit of a slog, and I personally couldn't connect with those moments. On the other hand, it isn't a bad movie by any means. 

In my opinion, I'd give this movie an **8.5/10**. 

It's definitely a movie I plan to re-watch once it's available on DVD.
