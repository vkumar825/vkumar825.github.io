## Happy 4th of July! 🎉 🦅 🇺🇸 🦅 🎉

It's the 250th anniversary of the United States!

What better way to celebrate than with a review of a movie made for this day, Young Washington, as my first-ever blog post! 

![Young Washington poster](https://res.cloudinary.com/u0n8mltb/image/upload/v1783210717/young-washington_k5yuct.webp)

The movie is directed by Jon Erwin, and while I am not familiar with this director or his previous works, I do recognize some members of the cast, in particular Andy Serkis and Ben Kingsley.

What I wanted to get out of this movie was seeing well-written characters, along with tons of action and epic battles throughout the movie.

Having seen the movie, I can say that it has met my expectations. At the beginning of the movie, it started off with an action sequence without any buildup, and it was a battle that was already ongoing, with young Washington abruptly awoken by it. I could feel the tension and excitement as we see him navigate through the smoke, bullets, corpses, and so on.

Then it transitioned into the flashback sequence midway, going through the life of a much younger Washington. It was a little abrupt, but that aside, I was able to see the progression and development. The movie had established the starting point of the arc, with his dream to become a British officer but was rejected due to his colonial background. 

This progressed to him managing to get a chance, though he suffered a major setback by the French forces, almost to the point of wanting to quit, following the loss of his half-brother, someone whom he deeply looked up to. And yet, thanks to his mother's encouragement, he still persevered against all odds. Overall, I really liked the development of Washington in this movie.

Plus, the action scenes were great; the battles presented were thrilling to watch, with muskets galore!

Overall, I found the movie very enjoyable to watch. Was it historically accurate? Probably not, and it's a movie! If it looks cool, then I'm all for it, plus it doesn't entirely feel out of character. 

Since I had such a good time, I'd give it a **10/10**! It's definitely a movie I plan to buy once it's available on DVD to re-watch it.
